About Kirayo

Most things we own sit idle. Your neighbour needs one this weekend.

Kirayo (from kiraya, rent) is a peer-to-peer rental marketplace for Indian cities. Anyone can list the camera, drill, tent or console they rarely use, and anyone nearby can rent it for a day or a week, safely.

Area first

You pick your area once and see items from people close by. Fewer trips, quicker handovers, no maps or tracking needed.

Trust you can see

Every member verifies a government ID once. Deposits sit with Kirayo, not the owner. OTP codes and photos record every handover.

Use more, buy less

Renting a projector for one party beats buying one that lives in a box. Owners earn, renters save, cupboards get lighter.

One person keeps it fair

Kirayo has a single admin who approves every ID and every listing, and steps in when a renter and owner can't agree on a deposit. Decisions are based on the pickup and return photos, never on who shouts louder.
