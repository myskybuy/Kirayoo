How it works

    Choose your city and area once. We show what’s available near you.

  2. 02 Find the item

    Send a booking request in chat. The owner accepts or suggests other dates.

  4. 04 Confirm the summary

    See days, rent, delivery fee, deposit and total before anything is charged.

Questions people ask

Still unsure? Write to us.

How is the deposit amount decided?

The owner sets it when listing, usually close to the cost of repair or replacement. Admin checks it's reasonable before the listing goes live.

When do I get my deposit back?

Within 48 hours of the owner confirming the return. If the owner doesn't respond within 24 hours, the return is confirmed automatically.

What if the item is damaged?

The owner can claim an amount with photos. You can accept or dispute it. The admin compares pickup and return photos and decides the final refund.

Why do I need to verify my ID?

Owners are handing over things worth thousands. A one-time ID + selfie check keeps everyone accountable. Only the admin sees your document.

Can I pay the owner in cash?

No. Payments outside Kirayo aren't covered by deposit protection or dispute help.
