Who sees your ID?

Only the Kirayo admin, to approve it. Owners never see your document. They only see a green "ID verified" tick next to your name.

What happens next
  1. Status shows Pending while admin checks (usually within a few hours).
  2. Once Verified, you can pay and lock the dates.
  3. If Rejected, we tell you why and you can re-upload.
